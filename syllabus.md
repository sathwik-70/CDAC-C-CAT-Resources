## 🗂️ Course-Wise C-CAT Categories

The C-CAT is divided into three test sections. Depending on your chosen PG Diploma courses, you will need to clear one, two, or all three sections:

| Category | Sections to Appear | Target Courses |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Category I** | Section A | PG-DGi (Geoinformatics) |
| **Category II** | Section A + B | PG-DAC (Advanced Computing), PG-DMC (Mobile Computing), PG-DBDA (Big Data), PG-DITISS (IT Infrastructure & Security), PG-DAI (Artificial Intelligence), PG-DCSF (Cyber Security) |
| **Category III** | Section A + B + C | PG-DESD (Embedded Systems), PG-DVLSI (VLSI Design), PG-DHPCSA (High Performance Computing), PG-DIoT (Internet of Things), PG-DRAT (Robotics & Automation) |

## 📘 Detailed Syllabus Breakdown

### 🔹 SECTION A (60 Minutes - 50 MCQs)
*Mandatory for all categories. Focuses on Quantitative Aptitude, Logical Reasoning, English, and Computer Fundamentals.*

#### 1. Quantitative Aptitude
*   **Arithmetic:** Number Systems, Fractions & Decimals, Square & Cube Roots, Simplification, Averages, Problems on Ages, Ratio & Proportion, Percentage, Profit & Loss.
*   **Commercial Math:** Simple & Compound Interest, Time & Work, Time, Speed & Distance, Pipes & Cisterns.
*   **Probability & Counting:** Permutations & Combinations, Probability, Clocks & Calendars.

#### 2. Logical Reasoning
*   **Verbal Reasoning:** Series Completion (Numbers, Alphabets), Coding-Decoding, Blood Relations, Direction Sense, Seating Arrangement (Linear & Circular).
*   **Analytical Reasoning:** Puzzles, Syllogisms, Analogy, Data Sufficiency.

#### 3. English (Verbal Ability)
*   **Grammar:** Tenses, Prepositions, Articles, Active & Passive Voice, Spotting Errors, Sentence Correction.
*   **Vocabulary & Comprehension:** Synonyms & Antonyms, Idioms & Phrases, One-word Substitution, Reading Comprehension.

#### 4. Computer Fundamentals
*   **Hardware & Memory:** Generations of Computers, RAM, ROM, Cache Memory, Virtual Memory, Secondary Storage Devices.
*   **OS & Number Systems:** Basics of Operating Systems, Number System Conversions (Binary, Decimal, Octal, Hexadecimal).
*   **Networks & Safety:** Networking basics (LAN, WAN, TCP/IP, OSI Layers), Internet & Cybersecurity basics.

---

### 🔸 SECTION B (60 Minutes - 50 MCQs)
*Mandatory for Category II & III. Focuses on Computer Programming and Software Concepts.*

#### 1. C Programming
*   **Fundamentals:** Data Types, Constants, Variables, Operators (Arithmetic, Bitwise, Logical, Relational).
*   **Control Flow:** Decision Control (if-else, nested-if, switch-case), Loop Control (for, while, do-while, break, continue).
*   **Functions & Memory:** Recursion, Storage Classes (`auto`, `register`, `static`, `extern`).
*   **Structures & Pointers:** Arrays, Strings, Multi-dimensional Arrays, Pointer Arithmetic, Dynamic Memory Allocation (`malloc`, `calloc`, `realloc`, `free`), Structures & Unions.

#### 2. Data Structures
*   **Linear Data Structures:** Arrays, Singly Linked Lists, Doubly Linked Lists, Circular Linked Lists, Stacks (LIFO), Queues (FIFO).
*   **Non-Linear Data Structures:** Binary Trees, Binary Search Trees (BST), AVL Trees, Graphs (BFS and DFS traversals).
*   **Algorithms:** Sorting Algorithms (Bubble, Selection, Insertion, Merge, Quick, Heap sort) and Searching Algorithms (Linear & Binary search).

#### 3. Object-Oriented Programming (OOPs) / C++
*   **Core Concepts:** Classes, Objects, Abstraction, Encapsulation, Data Hiding.
*   **Advanced OOPs:** Inheritance (Single, Multiple, Multilevel), Polymorphism (Function Overloading, Operator Overloading, Virtual Functions, Function Overriding), Access Modifiers (public, private, protected), Constructors & Destructors, Templates.

#### 4. Operating Systems
*   **Process Management:** States of Process, Threading, CPU Scheduling Algorithms (FCFS, SJF, RR, Priority).
*   **Concurrency & Deadlocks:** Semaphores, Critical Section, Deadlock Avoidance (Banker's Algorithm), Deadlock Prevention.
*   **Memory Management:** Paging, Segmentation, Virtual Memory, Page Replacement Algorithms (FIFO, LRU, Optimal).

#### 5. Emerging Technologies
*   **Big Data:** Definitions, Characteristics (5 V's), Hadoop Overview (HDFS, MapReduce).
*   **AI & ML:** Basics of AI (Turing Test), Machine Learning Categories (Supervised, Unsupervised, Reinforcement Learning).

---

### 🔺 SECTION C (60 Minutes - 50 MCQs)
*Mandatory for Category III. Focuses on Hardware, Electronics, and Low-level Architecture.*

#### 1. Digital Electronics
*   **Number Systems & Codes:** BCD, Gray Code, Excess-3 Code, Error-detecting codes.
*   **Combinational Circuits:** Boolean Algebra simplification, Karnaugh Maps (K-Maps), Logic Gates, Adders, Subtractors, Multiplexers, Decoders, Encoders.
*   **Sequential Circuits:** Latches & Flip-Flops (SR, JK, D, T), Excitation tables, Synchronous & Asynchronous Counters, Shift Registers.

#### 2. Computer Organization & Architecture (COA)
*   **Processor & Control Unit:** Machine Instructions, Addressing Modes, Instruction Cycles, Hardwired vs. Microprogrammed Control Units.
*   **Memory & I/O:** Cache Mapping (Direct, Associative, Set-Associative), Virtual Memory, DMA (Direct Memory Access), Interrupts, Pipelining hazards & speedup calculations.

#### 3. Microprocessors
*   **8085 Microprocessor:** Internal Architecture, Pin diagram, timing diagrams, 8085 Instruction Set, flag registers, Interrupt structure.
*   **8086 Microprocessor:** Register organization, Memory segmentation (Segment:Offset), basic Assembly Language programming.
