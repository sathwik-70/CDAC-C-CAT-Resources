## 💡 Pro Tips for Aspirants

1.  **Aptitude is a Rank Booster:** Do not ignore Section A. It is easier to score high here compared to Section B, which carries negative marking for tricky coding questions.
2.  **C Pointers and Output Guessing:** Focus heavily on pointer arithmetic, storage classes, preprocessor directives, and finding outputs in C programming.
3.  **Time Management:** You get 60 minutes for 50 questions per section. That is 72 seconds per question. Learn elimination strategies for MCQs.
4.  **Mock Tests:** Practice at least 5-10 mock tests online before the actual exam to get a sense of speed and negative marking management.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

<details>
<summary><b>1. What is the eligibility criteria for PG-DAC?</b></summary>
Candidates need a Graduate in Engineering (10+2+4 or 10+3+3 years) in IT/Computer Science/Electronics/Telecommunications/Electrical/Instrumentation, OR a Post Graduate Degree in Engineering Sciences (e.g., MSc in IT, Computer Science, Electronics), OR an MCA/MSc in Computer Science.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>2. How does the counseling and seat allocation work?</b></summary>
After receiving your C-CAT rank, C-DAC opens the choice-filling portal. You list your preferred courses and centers (e.g., PG-DAC at CDAC Pune). Seats are allocated in multiple rounds based strictly on your rank and choice preferences.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>3. Is there negative marking in the CCAT exam?</b></summary>
Yes. For every correct answer, you get <b>+3 marks</b>. For every wrong answer, <b>-1 mark</b> is deducted. Unattempted questions award 0 marks.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>4. Which centers are considered the best?</b></summary>
Historically, <b>CDAC ACTS Pune</b> and <b>CDAC Bengaluru</b> are considered top-tier due to their excellent placement records, infrastructure, and faculty.
</details>
