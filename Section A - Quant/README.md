# 🔹 Section A: Quant, Reasoning, English & Computer Fundamentals

This section is **mandatory for all candidates** attempting the C-DAC C-CAT exam. It consists of 50 questions to be solved in 60 minutes. Because it has a wide range of questions, securing high marks in Section A is crucial to boosting your rank.

---

*   **Key Topics:** Series Completion, Coding-Decoding, Blood Relations, Direction Sense Test, Syllogism, Seating Arrangements, Puzzles, Analogy, Data Sufficiency.
*   **Recommended Book:** *A Modern Approach to Verbal & Non-Verbal Reasoning* by R.S. Aggarwal.

## ⚡ Strategy Tips for Section A
*   **Pace Yourself:** You have roughly 1.2 minutes per question. If a logical puzzle or quant question is taking more than 2 minutes, **skip it** and return later.
*   **Start with Strengths:** If you are strong in Computer Fundamentals or English, solve them first. They take seconds to answer, giving you more time for Quant/Reasoning calculations.
*   **Learn Shortcuts:** Do not calculate things from scratch. Learn shortcuts for finding square roots, cube roots, compound interest, and ratios.
